SYS.00  PERSONAL ASSISTANT

A.R.E.S

A Really Expensive System

A.R.E.S is a private, personal desktop assistant. It runs on one person's own computer and shows a calm, heads-up-display style dashboard with the weather, computer status, a to-do list, calendar events and a brief of important emails. It can be spoken to, and it answers out loud.

Who it's for

A.R.E.S is a personal project, built for and used by its owner only. It isn't sold, offered to the public or available to download.

Google account access

When its owner signs in with Google, A.R.E.S uses:

  • Gmail, read-only: to list the owner's inbox, show emails and write short summaries. A.R.E.S never sends, deletes, labels or changes email.
  • Google Calendar events: to show the day's schedule and, only after the owner confirms each change on screen, add events or move and delete events that A.R.E.S itself created.

Details are in the privacy policy.
